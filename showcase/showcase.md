# Find your angle

Carom is a paddle duel with a twist in every return. Take the mint paddle against
a quick AI, or challenge a friend across the same keyboard. A bright ball cuts
through a dark court while two gold obstacles slowly sway and turn between you.

The opening serve gives you a moment to line up a return. Catch the ball near the
center to drive it straight, or reach with the edge to send it at a sharper angle.
Move through contact and your paddle adds spin: the ball bends away from the line
your opponent thought they were defending.

Those rotating bars are opportunities as much as hazards. A bank shot can turn a
safe rally into a scramble, and a curved return can find a gap just as it opens.
Every paddle hit picks up speed, so staying calm matters as much as moving fast.

First to eleven wins, but only with a two-point lead. At ten-all, one clever angle
isn't enough: make the return, read the bank, and earn the next one too.
